# ORG Brand Guidelines

*Draft. Plain-language guidance for using the Octagon Religious-Research Group (ORG) logo. Changes go
through the community vote.*

## The marks

- **ORG:** the name, as the Octagon Religious-Research Group.
- **The octagon mark:** the rainbow octagon used on orgspirituality.org and as the organization avatar.

Use the files in this repository, unmodified. Full name on first use in a piece:
**Octagon Religious-Research Group (ORG)**.

## ✅ Please do

- Use the official files from this repo (`avatar-512.png`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon-180.png`,
  `icon-192.png`, or the original `logo-original.jpeg`).
- Leave clear space around the mark, at least one eighth of its width on every side.
- Link back to orgspirituality.org or the ORG GitHub organization when you share the mark online.
- Describe ORG accurately: a member-governed community exploring spirituality and scientific research.

## 🚫 Please don't

- Stretch, crop into a different shape, recolor, rotate, add effects, or redraw the mark.
- Put the mark next to another organization's logo in a way that suggests endorsement or affiliation.
- Use the mark to suggest that ORG endorses a product, substance, provider, retreat, or event unless the
  community has voted on it.
- Sell merchandise bearing the mark without a community decision.

## Who may use the mark

- **ORG members and working groups:** yes, for ORG work.
- **Press and educators:** yes, to refer to ORG accurately.
- **Anything else** (merchandise, partnerships, commercial use): ask the community first, through
  orgspirituality.org.

## Questions

Ask through orgspirituality.org, or in the ORG community.
